Privacy Policy

Last updated 29 August 2026 · Dengteng LTD

This explains what this website does and doesn't do with your information. We've kept it short because most of it is simple: this is a small marketing website with no accounts, no forms, and no third-party trackers.

What we collect

  • If you email us or copy our business address from this site, that's information you've chosen to give us directly, and we use it only to reply to you.
  • If you arrive here through a marketing link (for example, one with a utm_source tag), the site remembers that tag for the rest of your browser session so we know how you found us if you get in touch. It's stored on your device only, is never sent to a third party, and disappears when you close the tab.
  • The site also stores one small flag in your browser once you dismiss the notice about the above, so we don't show it to you again. That's it.

What we don't do

  • We don't run any analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking scripts on this site.
  • We don't use cookies to identify or profile visitors.
  • This website doesn't include an AI chatbot or any AI-driven feature. (Our engineering work for clients does involve data science and machine learning — see our Services section — but that's separate from how this website itself operates.)
  • We don't publish testimonials or reviews on this site that aren't real, verifiable statements from an actual client.

Storage and security

We don't currently store any client files or personal data in cloud storage through this website — there's nowhere on the site to upload anything. If that changes in the future, any storage we add will be private by default, access-controlled, and encrypted, and we'll update this page before it goes live.
